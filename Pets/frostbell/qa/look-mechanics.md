# Look mechanics

Keep the feet, skirt, lower torso, wings and tail anchored in a frontal resting stance. The neck naturally pitches and yaws the head; curls, horns and the temple ornament follow the head. The face stays visible even at sideward gaze; no rear-facing turntable views. Preserve original large lavender irises, eyelids and highlights as coherent eyes.

Up: chin raised, nose lifted and irises visibly above eye centers. Down: chin lowered, upper eyelids lowered and irises below eye centers; eyes remain open. Right: head turns modestly toward screen-right, nose and both iris centers shift screen-right relative to head. Left is the inverse. Diagonals combine head pitch and yaw with both vertical and horizontal eye evidence. Intermediate poses progress evenly clockwise without reversing. The torso never rotates as a whole sprite.
